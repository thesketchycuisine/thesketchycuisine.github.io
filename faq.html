Questions guests ask about The Sketch-Y Cuisine

The Sketch-Y Cuisine is Ajmer's only hand-painted black-and-white restaurant: a 100% vegetarian restaurant and cafe in Vaishali Nagar, about 20 minutes from Pushkar, open daily from 11 AM to 11 PM.

What is The Sketch-Y Cuisine in Ajmer?

The Sketch-Y Cuisine is a 100% vegetarian restaurant and cafe in Vaishali Nagar, Ajmer, Rajasthan. Every wall, arch and ceiling is real 3D and hand-painted in black and white, so from the right angle the whole room looks like a flat pencil drawing. It is the only hand-painted black-and-white restaurant in Ajmer and Pushkar. The menu covers wood-fired and Neapolitan pizza, vegetarian sushi, dumplings, Pan Asian noodles, pasta, sizzlers, coffee and a full North Indian main course.

Is The Sketch-Y Cuisine a 2D cafe?

It is built on the same idea as the 2D cafes of Seoul and Tokyo, where a room looks like a drawing you can walk into. At The Sketch-Y Cuisine every wall, arch and ceiling is real and three-dimensional, painted by hand in black and white, so from the right angle the whole room looks like a flat 2D sketch, and when you move it turns back into a real room. It is the only hand-painted black-and-white restaurant in Ajmer and Pushkar.

Where exactly is The Sketch-Y Cuisine?

The address is 5th Floor, Baba Square, K-572, Makarwali Road, G Block, Vaishali Nagar, Ajmer, Rajasthan 305004, India. There is a lift to the 5th floor. Some food apps show the 4th floor because they follow the licence paperwork; the restaurant itself is on the 5th floor of Baba Square.

How far is The Sketch-Y Cuisine from Pushkar?

The Sketch-Y Cuisine is about 20 minutes by car from Pushkar, in Vaishali Nagar, Ajmer. It is a sit-down vegetarian restaurant, so it works well for an unhurried lunch or dinner on a day trip between Ajmer and Pushkar.

Where can I eat sushi in Ajmer?

The Sketch-Y Cuisine in Vaishali Nagar serves vegetarian sushi in Ajmer: Tempura Asparagus Cream Cheese, Avocado Uramaki and Rainbow Roll (6 pieces each), plus a Japanese Platter. It also serves dumplings, Korean Corn Cheese Ramen, Udon, Soba and Pad Thai noodles.

Where can I get wood-fired or Neapolitan pizza in Ajmer?

The Sketch-Y Cuisine bakes authentic Neapolitan-style pizza in a wood-fired oven, including sourdough Neapolitan pizzas such as The Sketch-Y Pizza (chef's special), Pesto Cherry Tree, Caramelised Onion and Margherita. Wood-fired pizzas are served Monday to Friday from 5 PM, and Saturday and Sunday from 12 noon.

Is The Sketch-Y Cuisine pure vegetarian?

Yes. The Sketch-Y Cuisine is 100% vegetarian and does not serve alcohol. Healthier choices include smoothie bowls, salads, grilled tofu and sautéed vegetables.

Does The Sketch-Y Cuisine serve Indian food too?

Yes. Along with pizza, sushi and Pan Asian food, The Sketch-Y Cuisine serves a full North Indian main course: dals such as Dal Maharani and Rajasthani Dal Panchmel, paneer curries, koftas, Jodhpuri Gatta Curry, biryani, tandoori tikkas and kebabs, and fresh breads from the tandoor.
